# HausFind PNG — Privacy Policy Draft

**Draft only — replace the placeholders and publish a publicly accessible policy before a public launch.**

HausFind PNG is a real-estate listing and inspection-request prototype.

## Data in this prototype
When you enter a viewing request or submit a property, the prototype stores the information locally in the app/browser on the device you are using. It does not send those details to an agency server in this version. Clearing app/browser data may remove saved records. Do not enter sensitive or confidential information.

## Facebook and third-party services
This prototype is not connected to Facebook or other social networks and does not retrieve listings from them.

## Future production service
A production version may need to process contact details, property information, appointment preferences and messages to provide agency services. Before collecting data centrally, the operator must update this policy to identify the legal operator, data collected, purposes, lawful basis/consent where applicable, recipients/service providers, retention periods, security controls, international transfers, user rights and contact details.
