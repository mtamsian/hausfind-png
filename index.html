Operating checklist

1. Confirm owner identity and authority to advertise.

2. Check the property details and availability.

3. Get permission before sharing a phone number or address.

4. Contact owner to agree on a viewing time.

5. Update appointment status and follow up with the customer.

6. Mark a listing unavailable once rented or sold.

Prototype limitations: no staff login, server database, automatic reminders, Facebook connection, or multi-device sync. Anyone with access to this browser may see its saved records. Do not store sensitive identity documents or payment information here.
